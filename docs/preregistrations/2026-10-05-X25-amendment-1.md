## What Stage 1 established

A single 1e-4 step per 256-token chunk does not move top-200 sampling — the
updates execute (4 per arm) and change weights bitwise (unit-tested), but no
sampled token flips on any of 20 stories. The mechanism is inert at these
settings, not broken. Stage 2 therefore sweeps **update strength**, not story
count: the variance is measured and small, the delta is zero for lack of
strength, not lack of stories.

## Stage 2 arms (all else frozen: seed 7, temp 0.8, top_k 200, 20 stories)

| arm | chunk_tokens | ttt_steps | ttt_lr | rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| control | — | — | 0 | identical code path, no update |
| strong-steps | 256 | 4 | 1e-4 | 4× the update volume, same strength per step |
| strong-lr | 256 | 1 | 1e-3 | 10× the step size |
| tight-chunks | 128 | 2 | 1e-4 | more frequent, fresher context |

All three treatments share the registered reset-per-story and mlp.down-only
rules. No other constant moves. If none separates from control, Stage 2
reports the mechanism insensitive at 58M across a 40× strength range — a
closed null, not a tuning invitation.

## The bar, set from measured variance

Story-level retention stdev is 0.0352 (n=20 stories, paired by story).
Standard error of the mean paired delta ≈ 0.008. The bar:

- **Primary:** mean paired delta (treatment − control) ≥ **+0.02** (≈2.5 SE),
  with the gain positive on a majority (≥11/20) of stories. Both conditions;
  a mean carried by outliers is not a mechanism.
- **Secondary (no bar):** mean max-span ratio per story, same pairing —
  recorded to judge whether retention is too sparse an instrument.
- **Guardrail:** val loss on the canonical set with the winning procedure
  applied must not regress beyond the Step-1 spread; until the spread is
  measured, reported without a verdict.

## Falsification carried forward

The LR=0 control and reset-per-story rules stand. Added: if strong-lr
improves retention but degrades fluency (repetition, collapse — judged by
reading 3 samples blind), the gain is instability, not memory, and Stage 2
reports it as such rather than as a pass.
