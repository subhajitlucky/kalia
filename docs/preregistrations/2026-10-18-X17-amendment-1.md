# Pre-registration — X17 Amendment 1: a second seed, and a bar that is not read alone

Registered: 2026-10-18, before any run of X17 exists.
Amends: `docs/preregistrations/2026-09-28-X17-doc-mask.md` (ledger `2a248036e6bcf69e…`).
Related: I15 (the defect), D15, **D48** (why this exists), X21 (the design it borrows).

## Why this amendment exists

X17 was registered on 2026-09-28. **D48 landed the next day** and measured
something no arm in this project had: the 30M control's own seed spread,
**0.1139 nats**. Its rule is explicit — *"No further architecture arm may be run
from a single seed. Minimum design going forward: paired control and treatment at
≥2 fresh seeds, interleaved."*

X17 as registered is a single-seed arm at **seed 1337**, which D48 also
identified as the seed that generated every prior hypothesis, and which X21
excluded from its own test as circular. Its DM-1 bar of **0.010 nats** descends
from D15's precedent, and D48's measurement puts that number an order of
magnitude below the ruler: a DM-1 pass *or* a DM-1 fail at one seed would be
unreadable in either direction.

This is not a flaw in X17's reasoning. It is that the registration is one day
older than the measurement that governs it. The question, the configs, the
metric and the thresholds are all unchanged. What changes is the design required
to read them.

The same D48 measurement is uncomfortable for the precedent DM-1 cites: D15
rejected Muon+ on a −0.015 effect against a 0.010 bar, and 0.015 is also far
below 0.1139. That rejection is reclassified as unreadable by D48 and is **not**
reopened here — it is recorded because it is the reason 0.010 is not treated as a
sound number to inherit.

## What changes

**1. Four arms, paired and interleaved, one kernel** — the X21 design, so the
arms share a machine and a session:

| Arm | Config | Seed |
|---|---|---|
| control | `micro-base` (`doc_mask: false`) | 1338 |
| **treatment** | `micro-docmask` (`doc_mask: true`) | 1338 |
| control | `micro-base` (`doc_mask: false`) | 1339 |
| **treatment** | `micro-docmask` (`doc_mask: true`) | 1339 |

Seeds 1338/1339 are the fresh pair D48 introduced and X21 used. **Seed 1337 is
not run.**

**2. The thresholds.** DM-1's number is unchanged, on the original's own
principle — *"we are not moving the goalposts for a fix we already believe in."*
What is added is that the number is no longer read alone:

| ID | Prediction | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **DM-1** | Treatment beats control on val loss | mean paired Δ ≤ **−0.010** (unchanged) |
| **DM-1b** | *new* — it is not one seed moved | **both** individual deltas negative |
| **DM-2** | Effect visible early, not only at the end | treatment below control at ≥3 of 5 eval points, at **both** seeds |
| **DM-3** | Cost bounded | treatment step time ≤ 1.25× control (unchanged) |
| **DM-4** | The mask is engaging | mean attended fraction per document < 1.0 (unchanged) |
| **DM-5** | *new* — reported, not tested | the paired-difference spread at two seeds |

DM-1b exists because a mean can be met by one seed while the other moves the
other way, and that is a null: X21's R-2 exists to refuse exactly that, and X21
is what happened when it was not required.

DM-5 has no threshold and no verdict. D48's 0.1139 is the spread of control
*values* across seeds; a paired delta is expected to cancel much of it, and the
project has never measured the spread of a paired delta at 30M. Reporting it
gives the next micro-ablation a ruler of the right shape instead of an inherited
one of the wrong size.

**3. Unchanged.** The 500-step budget, the 30M architecture (6L × 384d, ctx 512),
the two configs, the metric (val loss at the final step plus the full curve), the
eval cadence (every 100 steps, 20 batches), and the decision rule's outcomes.

## Honest priors, re-recorded before the run

- The original's priors stand, including the one that matters: **DM-1 may well
  fail at 0.010.** A result in the −0.005 to −0.015 range remains the single most
  likely outcome, and a large fraction of the plausible range sits below the bar.
- **New, from D48:** with a paired design much of the seed effect should cancel,
  so the paired spread is likely far below 0.1139. If it is *not* — if the two
  seed deltas disagree wildly — then the effect is unreadable at this budget, and
  that is the finding rather than a reason to run more seeds.
- **The confound is unchanged and cannot be removed.** Micro context is 512, not
  1024, so the boundary-crossing rate is higher here than in the real model and a
  micro result likely **overstates** the full-scale effect. Registered now so it
  cannot be forgotten when reading the result.

## The correctness question is not the val question

DM-1 asks whether masking *improves val loss*. It does not ask whether the data
path is *correct*, and neither answer substitutes for the other. If DM-1 fails,
the finding is "masking does not measurably help at 30M / 500 steps", published
as a negative — it is not evidence that the unmasked path was right.

The original's decision rule sends a DM-1 pass to **v0.2.1**. That release is no
longer the target: it is blocked on the replay contrast (journal 2026-10-18). The
rule's outcome is unchanged; the release it names is superseded, and the flag
setting for the next full-scale run is a separate decision from this measurement.

## Cost

Four arms at 30M / 500 steps ≈ **1.5 GPU-h**, against the original's ~0.75. Still
the cheapest registered experiment in the project, and it is the only registered
one that addresses a defect every shipped model carries.

## Deviations

Any further change to arms, seeds, steps or thresholds requires another
hash-registered amendment. Results are reported regardless of outcome.
