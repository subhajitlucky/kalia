# Amendment 1 to v0.3.0 — spread estimation, benchmark noise floor, stopping rule, replication

Registered: 2026-10-05, **before any GPU time has been spent on v0.3.0 and
before the quota-gated sequence has launched**. The original registration is
hash-anchored at
`b5df3e1f2a5e3177558184c3c90cc5283f31aaaf`
(`docs/preregistrations/2026-09-29-v030-continual-update.md`).

Nothing below changes the four steps, the arms, or the fixed thresholds table.
It tightens how three of those thresholds can be read, adds one stopping rule
the original lacks, and fixes the launch order the quota reset made ambiguous.

## 1. Step 1 measures the spread with three seeds, not two

The original Step 1 runs two control arms (1401, 1402) and calls the result
"the 58M control's own seed spread". Two points give a range, not a spread
estimate: if 1401 and 1402 happen to land 0.02 nats apart, every threshold in
Steps 2–4 inherits an overconfident denominator, and if they land 0.15 apart,
Step 1's own failsafe fires on what may be a two-point accident.

**Amendment:** add a third control arm at seed 1403, same recipe, same 500
steps, paired into the Step 1 kernel(s). Cost ≈ 0.3h. The spread used by
Steps 2–4 is the **max pairwise absolute difference** across the three arms,
not the 1401–1402 difference alone.

The Step 1 failsafe is unchanged in form: if the three-point spread exceeds
0.15 nats, all downstream thresholds are re-derived against it rather than
against their own numbers.

## 2. Benchmark claims register a minimum detectable effect

The 2026-09-28 journal records that X18's F-3 passed on registered terms at
+1.20 pp while the measurement noise at `limit=500` is 1.99–2.24 pp — a
0.54σ "pass" that clears a 0.5 pp bar about half the time by construction.
Step 4 (Kautilya) will produce benchmark numbers, and without a floor it can
repeat X18 exactly: a pass that means nothing.

**Amendment:** no benchmark delta in v0.3.0 is claimed as an improvement
unless it exceeds **2.0 pp at the sample size actually run**, or the sample
limit is raised until the standard error puts the observed delta at ≥2σ.
Deltas below the floor are reported as "within measurement noise", never as
regressions or improvements. This binds Steps 2–4 and the model card alike.

## 3. The update schedule gets a plateau stopping rule

v0.2.0's val curve plateaued for the last 31% of its schedule (2.874–2.940
from step 3250 onward): a third of a full run bought nothing measurable. The
binding constraint on v0.3.0 is quota, not budget, and none of Steps 2–4
registers when to stop spending it.

**Amendment:** for every update arm, if held-out val loss does not improve
for more than **40% of that arm's update budget**, the arm stops early and
the unspent quota is banked for the replication arm in §5. The stopping
check reads the same val stream the arm already logs; it adds no new
measurement. An arm stopped this way is reported as "plateaued", not as
failed — the nulls in Steps 2–4 already have that status available.

## 4. Step 0 runs before anything else, with no exceptions

The original already calls Step 0 "a hard prerequisite". This amendment makes
the sequencing mechanical: **no Step 1–4 kernel launches until the CL-0 probe
has been executed against the real v0.2.0 checkpoint on Kaggle and has shown
measurable forgetting.** The registered expectation stands — a probe showing
*no* forgetting means the probe is broken, and the sequence stops until the
probe is fixed. The five execution notebooks are ordered so that a Step 0
failure costs one kernel, not the sequence.

## 5. The winning arm is replicated at a second seed before any claim

D48 (X20) established that the control is not seed-invariant (~0.11 nats of
baseline spread at 30M micro scale), and every treatment arm in Steps 2–4
runs at a single seed (1401). A treatment delta measured at one seed is
unreadable against a spread measured across seeds — the exact mistake that
voided X18, X19 and X20.

**Amendment:** whichever arm wins in Steps 2–4 is re-run once at a second
fresh seed (1404) before any improvement is claimed in the model card or
release notes. If the repeat does not reproduce the direction of the effect,
the result is reported as seed-sensitive, not as an improvement. The quota
for this comes from §3's banked remainder first, and from a fresh allocation
only if the bank is empty.

## 6. Pre-launch quota checklist (operational, not a threshold)

The original was written against 0.61h remaining with a 2026-10-03 reset.
Before launching:

1. Confirm the actual available quota — the four steps plus §1's third arm
   total ≈ 2.9h.
2. Confirm `kalia-publish-sources` has published `corpus/probe_val.bin` and
   `corpus/<source>.bin` (Status update 4), since Step 0 and Step 4 cannot
   run without them.
3. Confirm the Step 1 kernel pairs all three control arms so one launch
   failure does not silently halve the spread sample.
