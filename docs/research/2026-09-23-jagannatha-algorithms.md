# Jagannatha of Puri — Two Compilations

Status: research record; practice adopted; one pre-registration filed
Date: 2026-09-23
Sources: Jagannath Temple/Puri origin accounts (Madala Panji, Skanda Purana
traditions; temple histories); pre-registration literature (Hofman et al. 2023,

## The narrative (as recorded)

- King Indradyumna sought the hidden deity Nilamadhava, secretly worshipped by
  the Savara chief Viswavasu.
- His priest **Vidyapati** could not find the cave. He married Viswavasu's
  daughter, was taken blindfolded to the shrine — and **dropped mustard seeds
  along the path**. The seeds sprouted and revealed the route afterwards.
- The needed image came from a floating log. **Vishwakarma**, as an old
  carpenter, agreed to carve it on one condition: **no interruption until the
  work was finished**. The queen's anxiety broke the condition; the sculptor
  vanished — and the **unfinished deities** (without hands) were installed.
  The incomplete form became the sacred, definitive form.

## Compilations

### J1 — Mustard-seed trail → hash-anchored provenance

The trick is a trace that survives the journey so the route can be retraced
without the guide: a covert index, not a map. In this project that practice
already exists as the journal and decision ledger; the upgrade is
**hash-anchored pre-registration** — a dated prediction whose SHA-256 is
committed before results exist, so anyone can later verify the claim was made
in advance. First use: `docs/preregistrations/2026-09-23-X16-chakravyuha-reversal.md`.

Nearest modern relative: pre-registration in predictive modeling and the
`preregister` tooling. **Adoption, not invention** — cited.

### J2 — Unfinished by design → pre-registered stopping as identity

The deity's identity is its stopping point: the interruption that violated the
committed rule produced the definitive artifact, and later generations did not
"fix" it. Compiles to: **the stopping rule is part of the artifact's identity,
declared before training and not renegotiated after seeing the curve.**
Applies to every remaining KALIA experiment (KALIA v0.1.2's target-loss stop is
the first instance).

Prior-art verdict: pre-registered stopping exists in the literature; the
contribution is the discipline and the name, honestly framed.

## What we do not take

- The "hidden deity protected by a tribe, retrieved by social engineering" plot
  offers no training mechanism (J1 already captures its useful kernel).
- The 21-day/15-day inspection deadlines and ritual prescriptions: no
  computational content.
