## Why this exists

Every zero-shot number behind the v0.2.0 release was measured at `limit=500`,
where D45 measured lm-eval's standard error at **1.99–2.24 pp** — wider than
most of the movements being reported. The 2026-10-17 review recommended 2,000
samples; `eval_bench.py` changed its default on 2026-10-18, but no 2,000-sample
run was archived. This is that run.

Both checkpoints are re-measured because a 2,000-sample v0.2.0 against a
500-sample v0.1.2 would reintroduce the error bar the run exists to remove.

PIQA (1,838 validation items) and WinoGrande (1,267) use their full sets because
they are smaller than 2,000; the other three use 2,000. The reported standard
errors reflect the actual n.

LAMBADA perplexity: 168.21 (v0.1.2) → 170.89 (v0.2.0), +2.68.

**No movement reaches 2σ.** The largest is PIQA at z=1.08. The only task
outside the registered 1.0 pp allowance is LAMBADA, by 0.25 pp, and its own
z is −0.92.

## What this changes

**The ARC-Easy regression was a 500-sample artifact.** At 500 samples the
recorded movement was **−3.80 pp**; at 2,000 it is **+0.45 pp** (z=0.29). The
v0.2.0 card and the SYNTHESIS previously said the corpus change "cost 3.8
points on ARC-Easy." That is retracted. The 500-sample number stays in the
record with its ~2 pp error; it does not survive the larger sample.

**The HellaSwag gain was also a 500-sample artifact.** +3.00 pp at 500 became
**0.00 pp** at 2,000.

**The PIQA gain shrank but stayed positive.** +2.40 pp at 500 became
**+1.74 pp** at 2,000, z=1.08 — not significant.

**The LAMBADA regression shrank but still trips the registered rule.**
−2.20 pp at 500 became **−1.25 pp** at 2,000, z=−0.92. It is still worse than
the 1.0 pp allowance, so the **registered S-A rule still fails** — on one task,
not two.

## The registered verdict, precisely

The frozen `2026-09-28-v020-final-evaluation.md` was registered at 500 samples.
Its verdict does not change retroactively:

- **P-B PASS** — the held-out loss bar of 0.05 nats was cleared by 0.2285.
- **S-A FAIL at 500** — ARC-Easy (−3.80) and LAMBADA (−2.20) both exceeded
  the 1.0 pp allowance.

The 2,000-sample re-measurement, which D45 and the 2026-10-17 review asked for,
changes the picture:

- **S-A at 2,000 fails on LAMBADA only** (−1.25).
- ARC-Easy is **+0.45**, not a regression.
- No movement reaches 2σ.

v0.2.0 therefore remains an **experiment release, not a promotion** — it still
fails the registered stability rule on the higher-resolution measurement, but
the failure is one task at the edge of the rule and is not statistically
resolvable. The old "two regressions" story is no longer supported.

## The loss/accuracy lesson, restated

A **0.2285-nat** loss gain produced at most **+1.74 pp** on any task, no 2σ
accuracy movement, and **−1.25 pp** on LAMBADA. Loss and accuracy remain
separate instruments: optimising the loss did not move accuracy in proportion.
But the specific claim that the corpus change *cost* 3.8 points on ARC-Easy is
false at 2,000 samples, and the weak-point section that built on it is
corrected.
