### Why it failed

- **The tax is not only the state loss.** Pairs-only costs +0.123/+0.122
  nats vs control; the workspace adds +0.094/+0.054 over pairs; the
  state/diversity terms slightly reduce the tax vs the workspace arm
  (−0.014/−0.008). The pair data and the workspace are the costs; the total is
  34–41× the 0.005 allowance.
- **The composition gain over pairs is noise.** +0.028/0.000, against a +0.10
  bar. The `pairs` baseline also barely moves unseen (+0.011 average), and its
  seen accuracy is unstable across seeds (0.342 vs 0.540).
- **`number+place` is a confound.** It is 0.0 in 9/10 arms; the `pack_box`
  template's causal relation ("packed" → "bag holds") is unseen in training.
  Excluding it, rename+swap +0.067/+0.107, swap+negate +0.027/−0.020,
  rename+negate +0.020/+0.020. Still not a consistent +0.10. The confound
  changes the size of the composition claim, not the verdict.
- **The adapter beats the workspace.** The parameter-matched output MLP (no
  pair data) improves val by **0.26 nats** over control; the workspace at the
  same budget is **0.44–0.48 nats worse than the adapter** (and **0.18–0.22
  nats worse than control**). At 30M the extra parameters are better spent at
  the output.
- **Retention is at the floor** (0.000–0.001) for every arm; I-4 passes but
  does not measure retention. The swap sensitivity is small and inconsistent.

### Decision

**Close X40.** The registered rule is I-1 or I-3 fail → stop. Both fail, both
seeds. The GPU budget is not spent on a mechanism that costs language quality
and does not beat the data baseline on the capability it was designed for.

This is a mechanism-level null, not a seed accident: the seen gain is
consistent but does not transfer, the unseen gain is within seed noise, the
workspace is alive, and the matched adapter beats the workspace on val.

v0.3.1 remains the model. The next mechanism needs a new pre-registration. A
cleaner composition benchmark would hold the causal relation fixed across
train/eval and vary only the composition; that is a benchmark contribution, not
a scale-up of X40.
